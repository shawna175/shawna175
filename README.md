My research interests lie in **Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Explainable AI, Medical Image Analysis, and Self-Supervised Learning**. I enjoy working on AI systems that are not only accurate, but also interpretable, reliable, and useful in real-world settings.

## Featured Research

### [LumaRep](https://github.com/shawna175/LumaRep)
**Interpretable Lumbar Spinal MRI Classification via Self-Supervised Learning**

Research on supervised, self-supervised, and semi-supervised learning for three-class lumbar spinal MRI classification.

- BYOL, DINO, and STAC-based learning
- Grad-CAM and occlusion sensitivity for interpretability
- Best model: **BYOL-VGG16 — 95.63% accuracy**
- **Accepted at CSNT 2027**

### [CaneScan-XAI](https://github.com/shawna175/CaneScan-XAI)
**Explainable Ensemble Deep Learning for Sugarcane Leaf Disease Classification**

An explainable computer vision framework combining attention mechanisms, ensemble learning, and visual explanations for plant disease classification.

- SE and CBAM attention mechanisms
- Weighted soft-voting ensemble
- Grad-CAM-based explainability
- Best ensemble accuracy: **97.20%**
- **Accepted and presented at IEEE RAAICON 2026**

### [WheatHead-26M](https://github.com/shawna175/WheatHead-26M)
**Cross-Domain Wheat Head Detection and Counting on GWHD 2021**

A computer vision study comparing YOLOv8 and YOLO26 for wheat-head detection, counting, and generalization across unseen field domains.

- Cross-domain evaluation on GWHD 2021
- Detection, counting, and accuracy-efficiency analysis
- Best model: **WheatHead-26M — F1 0.763, mAP@0.50 0.762**
- Counting performance: **MAE 8.085, R² 0.855**
- **Accepted for presentation at IEEE WIECON-ECE 2026**
